How Scoring Works
6 dimensions.
One Confidence Score.
After every session, the AI analyses your complete transcript and scores you across 6 specific dimensions. Each is weighted differently. Here's exactly what each one measures:
🎯

Content Relevance 25%

Does your answer actually address the question? The AI checks whether your response covers the core ask, includes relevant context, and stays on topic.

Semantic similarity between question and answer, topic coverage, avoidance of tangents.
🏗️

Answer Structure 20%

Is your answer clearly organised? For behavioural questions, the AI specifically checks for STAR format: Situation → Task → Action → Result.

STAR pattern detection, logical flow, presence of setup → action → outcome arc.
🔊

Voice Clarity 15%

How easy is your speech to follow? The AI uses transcript accuracy as a proxy — unclear or mumbled speech produces more transcription errors.

Speech-to-text confidence scores, word error estimation, transcript completeness.
🎵

Voice Modulation 15%

Are you speaking at an interview-appropriate pace? Ideal is 110–150 WPM. Too fast = nervous. Too slow = loses attention. Rhythm variation matters too.

Words per minute (WPM), pause detection, rhythm variance throughout the answer.
🚫

Filler Word Control 15%

"Um", "uh", "basically", "like", "you know" — each tracked in real time. Interviewers notice these immediately. Fewer fillers = higher score.

Filler-to-total-word ratio. Tracked fillers: um, uh, like, basically, you know, sort of, kind of, literally, actually (overuse).
💡

Answer Depth 10%

Does your answer go beyond surface level? Rewards specific examples, quantified outcomes ("grew sales 20%"), and real understanding over generic statements.

Specificity analysis, presence of numbers/examples, response length vs question complexity.
How the Confidence Score is calculated
Each dimension is scored 0–100, then weighted and summed. Scores above 80 are interview-ready. The AI recalibrates feedback based on your role and question type.
Content 25% Structure 20% Clarity 15% Modulation 15% Fillers 15% Depth 10%
